# OWNER OVERRIDE of brief rule A3 (the opening). This replaces A3 items 1, 6 and 7.

- Do NOT open with "Good evening, friend, and welcome back to Bedtime Backstory."
- Line one of the narration is the **scene hook** itself, 60–100 words.
- Directly after the hook comes the **welcome and support block**, 3–4 sentences, in this spirit (vary the wording slightly; no contractions, no "!"):
  "Welcome to Bedtime Backstory. Before we begin, if you would like to help this little channel, please just tap the like button. It helps the algorithm share this video with more people, and leaving one small comment, even a single word, is the kindest support you could give. That is the only time I will ask tonight."
  (Optionally add the invitation to comment where they are listening from and their local time.)
- Then the **myth reframe** ("Not the ___ of the picture books…"), the **stakes** line, and the **three carry-questions** with their linking line, as in A3.
- Then the **settle cue** (head heavy, shoulders loose…), ending with a soft "So let us begin." or "Now, let us begin." before the first scene starts.
- All of this stays within about the first 450–550 words.
- Everything else in the brief is unchanged, including the soft one-sentence like/subscribe in the close (A6) and "Sleep well, friend. Good night."
