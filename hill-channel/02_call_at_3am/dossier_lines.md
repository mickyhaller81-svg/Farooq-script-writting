# Dossier lines: 02 Who Would You Call at 3 A.M.?

- 1937, Think and Grow Rich typed (sections "A loan from a son.", "The hands already out.", "Pulling on the same rope.", "Whose Blair are you?"): said the book came out in nineteen thirty-seven when Hill was about fifty-three, and its typing was paid for with a three-hundred-dollar loan from his son Blair; script states the records do not say what passed between them. DOCUMENTED.
- Blair's birth (sections "A boy born without ears.", "Whose Blair are you?"): said "Hill wrote that" Blair was born without ears, that he refused to accept his son would never hear, and that Blair came to hear; no other details added. HILL'S OWN ACCOUNT, Think and Grow Rich (1937), chapter two.
- c. age nine, mother's death (section "What a nine-year-old learns."): said only that Hill's mother died when he was nine; script explicitly declines to say who comforted him. DOCUMENTED.
- 1928, The Law of Success, lesson "Pleasing Personality" (section "Pleasing is not pretending."): said it was published in nineteen twenty-eight when he was about forty-five, named the lesson, said it is free at the Internet Archive (spoken citation), and paraphrased the idea that an attractive personality is built from habits, chiefly real interest in others. DOCUMENTED (publication); lesson idea paraphrased in general terms.
- 1928, The Law of Success, lesson "Co-operation" (section "Pulling on the same rope."): named the lesson and paraphrased the idea that people pulling together in good faith carry what none could carry alone, starting from willingness to help. DOCUMENTED (publication); lesson idea paraphrased in general terms. Master Mind not mentioned.
- Support book, not a Hill moment: Hugh Black, Friendship (1898), one paraphrased line (friendship is made and tended, not just found).
