Hundreds of contacts, but who would you actually call at three in the morning, and who would call you? This narrated lesson draws on Napoleon Hill's life, including the three-hundred-dollar loan from his son Blair that got his 1937 book typed, and on the Pleasing Personality and Co-operation lessons from The Law of Success (1928). It walks through simple habits of real friendship (showing up, giving time, being honest, going first) and ends with one short message, word for word, that you can send tonight.
