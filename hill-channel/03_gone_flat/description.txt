You keep calling yourself lazy, but what you may really be is flat. This narrated lesson draws on the Enthusiasm lesson in Napoleon Hill's nineteen twenty-eight book The Law of Success and turns it into small daily actions: how you sit, how your voice sounds, the first thing you touch when you start work, and how you greet people. It closes with one simple rule for the first five minutes of anything and one two-line task to do tonight.
