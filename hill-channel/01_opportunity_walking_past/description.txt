A calm, practical lesson on spotting the chances already near you: in your job, on your street, and in the skills you take for granted. It draws on the restarts in Napoleon Hill's life, the Initiative and Leadership lesson from his 1928 book The Law of Success, and his telling of the "three feet from gold" story, read here as a lesson about seeing. You get one question to ask tonight and a simple paper exercise to do before bed.
Narrated lesson inspired by Napoleon Hill's public-domain writings. Not affiliated with the Napoleon Hill Foundation.
