## Story / voice-over script

**1 — 12 Hours at the Office**
It's 9:40 PM. The report is still due. He rubs his forehead and thinks, *"Bas ek ghanta aur... phir ghar."*
His phone keeps buzzing: *"Kahan ho?? Bachon ki fees! Mujhe naya sofa chahiye. Tum kuch nahi karte!!"*
He works late every night so his family can live better than most people in the country ever will.

**2 — His Only Day Off**
Sunday. He takes the kids to the park. *"Abbu! Ek aur jhoola!"* He is exhausted but he pushes the swing again.
At home, she relaxes on the sofa in the AC, scrolling her phone, and tells her friends:
*"Tum hamein kabhi bahar nahi le jaate!"*

**3 — Everything Paid For. Still Not Enough.**
Two maids. A driver. Bags from the mall. The card bill — mall, salon, maids, dining — all **PAID**.
A lifestyle fewer than 1% of Pakistani women ever get. Still: *"Phir bhi kuch nahi milta!"*
And online: *"Some husbands are just useless 🙄 #ToxicHusband"* — for everyone to see.

**4 — Who Asks How He Is?**
Late at night he sits alone on the balcony with cold chai. Inside: *"Aur paise chahiye! Tum kisi kaam ke nahi!"*
His phone: a meme calling him an ATM, a message calling him an idiot, and *no new messages* from friends he no longer has time for.
*"Main bhi insaan hoon. Kabhi koi poochta hai main kaisa hoon?"*

**Closing line:** Behind the paycheck there is a person. Say thank you. Ask him how he is. His mental health matters too.

### Suggested caption
