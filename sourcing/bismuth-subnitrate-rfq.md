## Points to check with every supplier
- Pharmacopoeia grade (BP / USP / EP edition) and the CAS number used (1304-85-4 / 10361-46-3 / 10035-06-0 all appear)
- COA of a recent batch, plus GMP certificate / manufacturing licence. Ask whether they are the manufacturer or a trader.
- Heavy-metal limits (lead, arsenic, silver, copper), since it is going into veterinary medicine
- Whether they accept **D/P at sight**. Many Chinese suppliers ask for T/T advance on small orders, so expect pushback.
- FOB port, lead time, packing (4 × 25 kg fiber drums), whether a 100 kg sea shipment (LCL) is feasible, and quote validity
