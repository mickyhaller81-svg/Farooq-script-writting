## VICTIM
**Sylvia Quayle**, 34 (some reports say 35). Englewood High School graduate. Ran a small business, **"The Buttery"**, specialising in wedding cakes. "Ambitious, friendly, vibrant, and lit up the room when she walked in" (Chief Michelle Tovrea). Very close to her parents and younger sister. Had a regular custom of **morning coffee with her parents**. Her father, **William Quayle**, came to check on her when she didn't show, and found her. Her grave marker reads "beauty seen is never lost". [SRC 1, 2, 3]
Speaks for her: sister **Jo Hammit** ("turned my family's world upside down"; "For the past 41 years, Sylvia missed out on family celebrations…"). [SRC 4]
