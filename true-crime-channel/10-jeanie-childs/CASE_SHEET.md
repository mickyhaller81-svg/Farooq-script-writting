## VICTIM
**Jeanne Ann "Jeanie" Childs**, born April 21, 1958, to Eugene and Betty Eakman; 35. Doted on her little sister **Cindy** (Blumer/Kosnitch). Married a man with children and became their stepmother, "the only mother they really knew", and stayed in their lives after the split. Her family say she was happy, compassionate and individual. Left home young; later did sex work (state once, without judgement, after she is a person). [SRC 1, 2]
Family voices: mother **Betty Eakman** (hospice worker; learned of the murder from a patient's TV; "I've waited so many years…"); niece **Melissa Beaulieu**; sister **Cindy** ("My children have grown up never knowing their Aunt Jeanne. I am so sorry we never got to say goodbye."). [SRC 3]
