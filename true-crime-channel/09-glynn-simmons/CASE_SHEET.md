# CASE SHEET — 09 · Glynn Simmons

Status: RESEARCH v1 (2026-10-02). Web search only.

```
CASE:        Robbery-murder of Carolyn Sue Rogers, Edmond Liquor Store, Edmond, Oklahoma — Dec 30, 1974
             → wrongful conviction of Glynn Simmons → declared actually innocent Dec 19, 2023
CENTRAL Q:   How was a man who says he was in Louisiana convicted of a murder in Oklahoma, and how
             did one police report stay hidden for 48 years of his life?
TURN:        (~23:00) The lineup report: the only eyewitness had picked out other men, and the defence never saw it.
STRUCTURE:   Split screen. The same night told twice, in Edmond and in Harvey, Louisiana, and the gap between
             them carries the story. Differs from 01–08.
```

## VICTIM(S)
**Carolyn Sue Rogers**, 30, store clerk, shot as she picked up the telephone during the robbery. Sister: **Janice Smith**. Second clerk: **Norma Hankins** (ordered to open the register). Customer **Belinda Brown**, 18, shot in the head and survived; the only eyewitness. [SRC 1, 2]

## TIMELINE
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1974-12-30 | Robbery; Rogers killed; Brown shot and survives |
| 1975-02-07/08 | Lineups. Police bring in people from a party; Brown identifies Simmons and Roberts. A police report records that across eight lineups she also identified others (incl. "Delbert Patterson" and "subject #__"). |
| 1975-02-08 | Simmons and Roberts arrested |
| 1975 | Trial: 7 alibi witnesses say Simmons was in Harvey, LA; Brown testifies she identified no one else; June 5 convicted; July sentenced to death |
| 1977 | Death sentences modified to life after US Supreme Court rulings |
| ~1995 | The lineup report surfaces (~20 years later) [verify exact year] |
| 2008 | Roberts paroled |
| Decades | Parole repeatedly denied despite Pardon & Parole Board support at times |
| 2023-04 | DA Behenna moves to vacate (Brady: the withheld report) |
| 2023-07-18/20 | Vacated; new trial ordered; released on bond (age 70) |
| 2023-09-19 | Charges dismissed |
| 2023-12-19 | Actual innocence: "clear and convincing evidence" |
| 2024 | $175k state compensation; GoFundMe for cancer treatment (>$350k) |
| 2024-06 | DA declines Roberts's request |
| 2024-08 | Edmond settlement $7.15m |

Time served: 48 years, 1 month, 18 days, the longest known wrongful imprisonment in US history (National Registry of Exonerations).
