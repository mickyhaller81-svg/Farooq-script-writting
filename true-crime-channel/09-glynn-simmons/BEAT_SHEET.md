# BEAT SHEET — 09 · Glynn Simmons

- **Title:** 48 Years in Prison for a Murder in Another State (48 chars)
- **Alt:** The Page the Prosecutors Never Saw · The Longest Wrongful Imprisonment in American History
- **Structure:** Split screen. Two places, one night, Dec 30, 1974: Edmond, OK, and Harvey, LA. The script cuts between them, and the hidden report bridges them.
- **Tier C:** there is no perpetrator to withhold. Withheld element: the lineup report, revealed at the midpoint.
