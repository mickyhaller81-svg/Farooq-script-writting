# CASE SHEET — 06 · Iwao Hakamada

Status: RESEARCH v1 (2026-10-02). Web search only.

```
CASE:        Hashimoto family murders, Shimizu (now part of Shizuoka City), Japan — June 30, 1966
             → wrongful conviction of Iwao Hakamada → acquittal Sept 26, 2024
CENTRAL Q:   How does an innocent man spend 46 years waiting to be hanged, and what does it take
             for a country that convicts more than 99% of defendants to admit it was wrong?
TURN:        (~23:00) Thirteen months after the murders, five bloodstained garments "appear" in a miso tank
             at the factory, and the trousers are too small for him.
             (~38:00) The colour of blood in miso: the 2024 court rules the evidence was fabricated by investigators.
STRUCTURE:   Letters spine. Each act opens with a line from Hakamada's own prison letters (published 1992),
             tracking a mind from confidence to despair to silence. Differs from 01–05.
```

## TIMELINE
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1936 | Hakamada born (Shizuoka Prefecture) |
| 1959–61 | 29 pro bouts, featherweight; ranked 6th |
| 1966-06-30 | Fire at the Hashimoto home, across the tracks from the factory dormitory where Hakamada lived; four bodies found, stabbed. Hakamada says he helped fight the fire. |
| 1966-08-18 | Arrested (49 days later) |
| 1966-08–09 | Interrogated ~264 hours over 23 days, up to 16 hours a session (per his lawyers); confesses on day 20; later retracts |
| 1967-08 | Five bloodstained garments "found" in a miso tank at the factory (~14 months after the murders); trousers too small; prosecution says they shrank; "B" tag (said to be size) actually meant the colour black |
| 1968 | Shizuoka District Court: death sentence (Kumamoto in the minority, per his 2007 account) |
| 1980 | Supreme Court upholds; death sentence final |
| 1980s–2014 | Death row (Tokyo Detention House), much of it in effective isolation; execution notice comes only on the morning itself |
| 1992 | His *Letters from Prison* published |
| 2007-03 | Kumamoto: "Since I had convicted him, I felt extremely guilty…" |
| 2014-03-27 | Shizuoka District Court grants retrial and releases him; DNA did not match; possible fabrication; Murayama: "unbearably unjust…" |
| 2014-04 | WBC honorary championship belt |
| 2018-06 | Tokyo High Court reverses (he stays free) |
| 2020-12 | Supreme Court sends it back |
| 2023-03-13 | Tokyo High Court grants retrial |
| 2024-09-26 | Acquitted. Court: clothing evidence fabricated by investigators; interrogation-based confession inhumane; blood would not stay reddish after more than a year in miso |
| 2024-10 | Prosecutors don't appeal (Unemoto); police chief Tsuda apologises in person; senior prosecutor bows in apology |
| 2025-03 | Compensation: ¥217,362,500 (~$1.4m) = ¥12,500 per day |

## UNKNOWNS
Who killed the Hashimoto family. Exactly who planted the clothing. (The court attributed it to investigators; no individual has been named or charged.)
