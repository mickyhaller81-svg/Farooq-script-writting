## VICTIM
**Leslie Ann Jennings Preer**: born 1952, North Kingstown, Rhode Island. Grew up across the country in a large military family. Studied journalism at the University of Florida. Settled in Chevy Chase in 1982 with husband Sandy and daughter Lauren. Volunteered at a local library teaching English as a second language. Had a job (her employer helped raise the alarm). [SRC 1, 2]
Age: 49 in most reports (some say 48 or 50).
Speaks for her: daughter **Lauren Preer** (public statements; "a wolf in sheep's clothing" at sentencing) and family victim-impact statements (seven family members, Aug 2025). [SRC 3, 4]

## EVIDENCE SPINE
1. Male DNA under Leslie's fingernails (proof of a struggle) → excludes Sandy → Othram → Gligor.
2. The January 2002 tip in the file.
3. The Dulles water bottle → match → the interrogation tape → plea.

## UNKNOWNS
- Motive: prosecutors say none established and no evidence of premeditation. The defence cites a night of binge drinking and cocaine and says Gligor "blacked out".
- How he got in (back door unlocked; no forced entry).
- Why the 2002 tip wasn't followed up.
- Sandy's polygraph: secondary sources only. Frame as "reportedly".
