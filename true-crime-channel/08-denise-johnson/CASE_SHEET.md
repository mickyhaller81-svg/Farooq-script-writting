## CONTEXT FOR THE EDITORIAL LAYER (§10.2)
- Palo verde: Arizona's state tree, with green photosynthetic bark and bean-like seed pods.
- 1935 Lindbergh/Hauptmann trial: wood expert Arthur Koehler traced the ladder wood, the famous earlier botanical evidence.
- 1993 Daubert v. Merrell Dow (June 1993) replaced Frye in federal courts. Arizona then used Frye.
- RAPD later superseded by more robust plant markers. This was a first, not a template.
