## 9 · THE BREAK (37:00–43:00 · 840 w) — SLOW
- **PAY the withheld detail from block 6:** her husband was **Gerald "Jerry" Nemke**. In 1960, aged 17, he was convicted in Chicago of the murder of a 16-year-old girl, **Marilyn Duncan** (named once, no detail). Sentenced to death; the Illinois Supreme Court reversed in 1962; retried and convicted again. Released from prison in **1978**. (Court records: People v. Nemke. Tier 1.)
- Married Lee in 1978, divorced 1979, remarried December 1981, six months before Room 106. (State facts; no narrator inference.)
- **PAY "why Carbondale":** after prison, Nemke went to college in Carbondale, about fifteen miles from where Freeman was buried. Authorities have said the two men were previous acquaintances.
- **[CONDITIONAL, pending verification]:** the prison link, meaning a man who served time alongside Nemke was reported to be a friend and co-worker of Freeman.
- **§7 frame, said explicitly:** Nemke had an alibi for June 25 and it held. He was never charged in Lee's death or Freeman's. He died in 2019. Investigators have since named him a **person of interest in Freeman's death**, and detectives have said they suspect a hired killing. **That is their theory; no court has tested it.**
- **OPEN (block Q):** If they're right, the case has two killers. Only one of them has ever been named by evidence.

## 10 · RESOLUTION (43:00–49:00 · 840 w)
- **Spine restated (~43:00):** who killed Lee is answered. Why is not.
- Feb 25, 2022: Capt. Weddum announces the case **cleared**: "It took this new technology to solve this case." What "cleared by exception" means when the suspect is dead: no charge, no trial, no defence. (Editorial layer.)
- **The defence's argument, honestly (§4 block 10):** with no trial, the counter-case is the record itself. Nemke's 1982 alibi held, no court has heard the hire theory, and a dead man cannot be cross-examined. State it fairly.
- Illinois State Police and Council Bluffs: a joint look at Freeman's murder. As of 2025 it is still open.
