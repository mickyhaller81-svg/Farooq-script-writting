# CASE SHEET — 01 · Lee Rotatori / Thomas Freeman

Status: RESEARCH v1 (2026-10-02). Not yet cleared for prose: see "Open verification items" at the bottom.

```
CASE:            Murder of Lee Gunsalus Rotatori — Best Western Frontier Motor Lodge, Room 106,
                 2216 27th Avenue, Council Bluffs, Iowa — June 25, 1982
                 (Pottawattamie County case no. F82-2558)
                 Linked: murder of Thomas Oscar Freeman — Cobden, Illinois — c. July 1982, found Oct 30, 1982

CENTRAL Q:       Who killed Lee Rotatori in a locked motel room on her first week in a new city —
                 and why did the answer, forty years later, open a second murder instead of closing one?

ANGLE (§10.1):   The DNA answered "who" in 2021. It could not answer "why" — because the man it named
                 was murdered himself a few weeks later, and the trail of "why" runs back to the one
                 person police cleared on day one.

THE TURN:        (~23:00) The genealogy names Thomas Oscar Freeman, a trucker from West Frankfort, Illinois —
                 and detectives learn he has been dead since the summer of 1982, shot and buried in a
                 shallow grave. Second turn (~37:00): the cleared husband had served 18 years for murder
                 in the same prison system as a friend and co-worker of Freeman.
```

## VICTIM

**Lee Gunsalus Rotatori**, 32 (born 1949), of Nunica, Michigan.
- Had just been hired as **food service director at Jennie Edmundson Hospital**, Council Bluffs; staying at the motel until she found a home. [SRC 1, 2, 4]
- As a girl in Rochester, Minnesota, she **loved drawing horses**; as an adult in Michigan she finally **owned her own horse**. [SRC 5]
- She and her brother Greg were on the **Rochester junior rifle club**. [SRC 5]
- Siblings describe her as "mostly happy and outgoing, an artistic woman with lots of friends." [SRC 5]
- Kept the surname of her first marriage for professional reasons; her **11-year-old son** lived with her first husband near Chicago. [SRC 5]
- Last evening: a few hours **boating with new hospital co-workers**, broke up at dusk; stopped at **McDonald's — a meal for one** — back to her ground-floor room unseen by motel staff. McDonald's employees were the last known people to see her alive. [SRC 6]

**Who speaks for her:** sister **Ann Chinn** (Rochester, MN, where Lee is buried) and brother **Greg Gunsalus** — public statements to The Gazette, 2022. [SRC 5]

## EVIDENCE SPINE
1. **The crime-scene DNA** (1982 → 2001 profile → no hit for 18 years → genealogy 2019–21 → daughter's sample).
2. **The scene itself**: no forced entry, a single wound, missing purse/jewelry with an un-ransacked room — the details that made 1982 police think of a targeted killing.
3. **The Illinois file**: Freeman's shallow grave, ~15 miles from where Lee's husband had gone to college; the prison acquaintance linking the two men.

## WHAT IS GENUINELY NOT ESTABLISHED (UNKNOWNS)
- Who killed Thomas Freeman.
- Whether anyone hired Freeman. The contract theory is **investigators' suspicion as reported**, never tested in court.
- Whether Freeman and Lee had any contact before that night.
- Exact date Freeman died (reported as ~July 1982; "31 days later" in the topic file is **NOT supported** — do not use).
- What happened to the purse and jewelry.
